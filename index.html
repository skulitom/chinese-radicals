About this Mandarin practice tool

A free, browser-based app for drilling Chinese characters and radicals. Includes the full set of 214 Kangxi radicals (the building blocks of every Hanzi) and 477 common characters covering the first few hundred most-used words. Each item is paired with its pinyin (with tone marks) and a concise English meaning.

Switch between Simplified (国, 学, 来) and Traditional (國, 學, 來) characters at any time — mastery progress is shared across the two forms. Choose your quiz mode (match to pinyin or to meaning) and deck (radicals or characters) from the header. Items you miss come up more often, and three correct answers in a row marks a character as mastered. All progress is stored locally in your browser — no signup, no tracking.

Open source on GitHub. Issues and contributions welcome.
